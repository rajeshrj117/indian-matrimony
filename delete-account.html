Delete your account

Indian Shaadi Matrimony (com.indianshaadi.matrimony)

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in. If you signed in a while ago, sign in again, because deletion needs a recent sign-in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Safety.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. You can also tap Download my data on the same screen first if you want a copy.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot open the app, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the subject "Delete my account". Write from the email address you registered with, or include your registered mobile number, so we can confirm the account is yours. We complete requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and private details (phone, email, date of birth, location, horoscope details)
  • Your profile photos and verification selfies
  • Your likes, interests, shortlists, profile views, matches and messages
  • Your notifications, push tokens and sign-in record

What we may keep

For safety and legal reasons we may keep reports filed against an account. If an account was suspended or banned, we also keep its phone number or email on a block list so it cannot re-register.
