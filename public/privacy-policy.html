Privacy Policy

Indian Shaadi Matrimony (com.indianshaadi.matrimony)
Last updated: 5 October 2026

Indian Shaadi Matrimony ("the app", "we") helps adults in India find a life partner. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The app is operated by Rajesh L, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

Who can use the app

The app is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth to confirm this, and only your age is shown to others. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details: your mobile number (verified by OTP) or your Google account name and email.
  • Profile details you enter: name, date of birth, gender, who the profile is for, marital status, height, religion, community, mother tongue, education, occupation, income, diet, smoking and drinking habits, state, native place, family details, bio, interests and partner preferences.
  • Horoscope details (optional): star, rashi, manglik status, birth time and birth place.
  • Photos: profile photos you upload.
  • Verification selfie: a live selfie taken with your camera to confirm you are the person in your photos.
  • Location (only if you allow it): your device location, used to show nearby profiles.
  • Messages and activity: chats with your matches, interests sent and received, shortlists, profile views, notifications, and reports or blocks you make.
  • Device and technical data: push notification tokens, IP address and usage signals used to prevent abuse and keep the app working.
  • Payment information: if you buy Premium, payment is handled by our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not see or store your card or UPI details.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, show your profile to other members and suggest matches.
  • To verify profiles and detect fake accounts, scams, explicit content, harassment and duplicate photos.
  • To deliver messages and notifications.
  • To respond to reports, support requests and legal obligations.
  • To process payments for Premium.

What other members can see

Other signed-in members can see your name, age, photos (subject to your photo privacy setting), bio, matrimony details, star/rashi/manglik, verified badge, city-level location (about 10 km precision) and online status (you can hide last seen).

They cannot see your phone number, email, date of birth, exact location, birth time, birth place, verification selfie, blocked list, or who you reported.

Automated checks and AI providers

To keep the community safe, your photos, selfie and messages are checked by automated systems:

  • Google Gemini compares your verification selfie with your profile photo and screens photos for nudity, violence, minors and similar issues.
  • Groq screens chat messages for scams, threats, explicit content and similar violations, and powers features such as bio and compatibility suggestions.

These providers process the content only to return a result to us. Moderators may review reported conversations.

Who we share data with

We use service providers to run the app: Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, push notifications and hosting), Google Gemini, Groq, and Razorpay for payments. We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted

Messages are encrypted in transit and stored with our cloud provider, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.

Safety and moderation

Accounts that break our rules may be restricted or banned. For banned accounts we keep the phone number or email on a block list so the person cannot simply re-register.

Retention and deleting your account

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • You can download your data and delete your account any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & Safety. See how to delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, verification selfies, likes, matches, messages, notifications and sign-in records from our systems.
  • We may keep limited records needed for safety or legal reasons: reports filed against an account, and the block-list entry of a banned account.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: verification selfie and profile photos.
  • Photos / files: choosing profile photos.
  • Location (optional): showing nearby profiles.
  • Notifications: alerts for messages, interests and matches.

Your rights

You can access, correct, download and erase your data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. Write to the contact below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and notify you in the app for significant changes.
