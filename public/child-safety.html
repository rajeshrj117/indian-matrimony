Child Safety Standards

Indian Shaadi Matrimony (com.indianshaadi.matrimony)
Last updated: 5 October 2026

Indian Shaadi Matrimony has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). The app is for adults aged 18 and over only. This page explains how we prevent, detect and respond to CSAE on our platform.

Adults only

  • Every member must enter a date of birth and confirm they are 18 or older to create a profile.
  • Profiles of people under 18 are not permitted. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we remove it.
  • Profiles created by family members are only for adult relatives who are 18 or older.

Prohibited content and behaviour

We prohibit any content or conduct that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including CSAM, grooming, soliciting or sharing sexual content involving minors, and any attempt to contact minors. Accounts that do this are permanently banned.

How we detect it

  • Uploaded photos are checked automatically for nudity, violence and signs that a person may be a minor, and for duplicate or reused photos.
  • Chat messages are screened automatically for sexual content and other violations.
  • Human moderators review reported profiles, photos and conversations.

How members can report

Every profile and chat has a Report option and a Block option. Reports are confidential. You can also email us directly at the address below.

How we respond

  • Content that may involve CSAE is removed immediately and the account is suspended or permanently banned.
  • For banned accounts we keep the phone number or email on a block list so the person cannot re-register.
  • We preserve the relevant evidence and report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the appropriate authorities, including Indian law enforcement and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), in line with applicable law, including the POCSO Act, 2012.
